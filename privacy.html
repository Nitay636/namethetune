Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Name the Tune is made by Nitay Shporn. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

No accounts, no tracking, no ads

The app has no accounts of its own, shows no ads, and does not track you across apps or websites. It contains no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Games over the internet

When phones play together over the internet, the game goes through a Google Firebase Realtime Database. While a game runs it holds the game's 4-digit code, the names players type, the songs in the game, buzzes, answers and scores, and a random ID for each phone. No email, phone number, location or account details are sent. It is only reachable with the game's code and is used only to run the game. Games between nearby iPhones go directly between the phones.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you choose to say your answer, the app listens only while you answer and uses your phone's speech recognition (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) to turn it into text. The app does not record or keep audio.

Spotify and Apple Music

If you connect Spotify or Apple Music, the app uses them only to load playlists, search and play songs. Sign-in details stay on your phone. Their own privacy policies apply to your use of them.

On your phone

Your settings, your name and your game history are kept only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
