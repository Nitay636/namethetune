Name the Tune

A party game: hear the start of a song, buzz first, name it. Everyone plays on their own phone.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Open a support request and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

What do I need to play? The host needs Spotify (Premium) or Apple Music to play the songs. The other players only need the app: they tap Join a game and type the game's code, or join a nearby iPhone.

Can I try it without Spotify? Yes. Turn on Demo mode in Settings to play a demo game without music.

Why does the app ask for the microphone? After you buzz you can say your answer instead of typing it. It's only used while you answer.
